Zones pays
attention.

Choose the heart rate you want to ride near. As your monitor reports back, Zones adjusts the trainer’s power target.

  1. Make the connection.

    Pair a compatible Bluetooth trainer and heart-rate monitor. Check what your setup needs.

  2. Give the ride a target.

    Your chosen heart rate guides the adjustments. It’s a target to ride near—not an exact number Zones can guarantee.

Same watts.
Not always the
same feeling.

You’ve settled into the ride. The power is steady. But your heart rate can still drift upward. That’s where heart-rate-led control takes a different approach.

Your body responds.

Heart rate reflects a changing response to the ride. It doesn’t move in lockstep with the number on your trainer.

The target can respond, too.

As heart rate rises, Zones can ease the power target. Your heart rate takes time to follow; adjustments are gradual, and responses vary.

Then, a moment to look back.

Completed-ride history gives you somewhere to revisit the session. A reference for your next ride, not a verdict on this one.

Bring the bike.
Check the
connections.

The idea is simple. The right hardware still matters.

A trainer that can take a target.
Bluetooth FTMS is the starting point. Target-power control, a valid power range and the app’s control checks must also be supported. FTMS alone does not guarantee compatibility.
A monitor that keeps reporting.
A Bluetooth heart-rate monitor using the Heart Rate Service (HRS), with live heart-rate readings available to Zones.

Specific equipment is not yet verified for publication. Hardware compatibility is separate from account integrations; automatic uploads to third-party services are not part of this offer.
