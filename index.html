Same power.
Different effort.

A steady number on your trainer doesn’t always mean a steady effort. As a ride goes on, your heart rate can drift. Zones uses that changing signal to adjust the power target—not just hold the same watts.

  1. Connect your setup.

    Pair a compatible Bluetooth trainer and heart-rate monitor.

  2. Choose your target.

    Set the heart rate you want to ride near.

  3. Let the ride respond.

    Zones adjusts the trainer’s power target as your heart rate changes. You keep pedalling.

The right
connections.

Heart-rate-led control needs two things: a trainer that can accept a power target, and a monitor that can report your heart rate.

A compatible smart trainer.
Bluetooth FTMS support is the starting point—not a guarantee. Target-power control, a valid power range and the app’s control checks must also be supported.
A heart-rate monitor.
A Bluetooth monitor using the Heart Rate Service (HRS), with live heart-rate readings available to Zones.
A connection, not an account.
Bluetooth hardware compatibility is separate from third-party account integrations. Automatic uploads to other services are not part of this prototype’s offer.
